# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be considerate, focus criticism on ideas and code, respect privacy, and assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/georgezouq/fylune/security/advisories/new) with the subject “Code of conduct”. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
